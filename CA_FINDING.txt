1. Extract the live node certificate

openssl s_client -connect NODE_IP:9300 -showcerts </dev/null 2>/dev/null \
  | openssl x509 -out /tmp/live-node.crt

2. Test every CA you have

for ca in /path/to/cas/*.crt; do
    echo -n "$ca: "
    openssl verify -CAfile "$ca" /tmp/live-node.crt 2>/dev/null
done

The CA that returns:

/path/to/cas/the-right-one.crt: /tmp/live-node.crt: OK

is the CA that signed the certificate currently being presented by the node.
